# My mistakes log

Write 1–3 lines at the end of every session. Write down anything you got wrong, needed `hint()` or `answer()` for,
or found confusing. On review days, redo these and put ✅ next to the ones you can now do alone.

This log is your most valuable file: it shows exactly where your weak spots are.

**Format:** `topic #number: what went wrong → what's right`

Example:
- pandas #14: used `.count()` to total the visits → `.count()` counts non-empty cells, `.sum()` adds the values ✅
